Theoretical Foundations:
• Little's Law: \( L_q = \lambda \cdot W_q \) and \( L = \lambda \cdot W \)
• Traffic Intensity: \( \rho = \frac{\lambda}{c \mu} \). If \( \rho \ge 1.0 \), the queue is mathematically unstable and grows indefinitely.
• Erlang-C Delay Probability: \( P_q = \frac{\frac{(c\rho)^c}{c!(1-\rho)}}{\sum_{k=0}^{c-1} \frac{(c\rho)^k}{k!} + \frac{(c\rho)^c}{c!(1-\rho)}} \)
• Allen-Cunneen M/G/c Correction: \( W_q(M/G/c) \approx W_q(M/M/c) \cdot \frac{C_a^2 + C_s^2}{2} \)
