Why queues get long

Demand is uneven

Hospitals, universities and government offices see rushes at opening time, around deadlines and after lunch. A fixed rota cannot follow them.

Counters are not equal

Some staff are faster, some desks handle harder cases. Sending customers to counters in turn ignores that, so one queue crawls while another sits idle.

Queue tokens only hide the problem

An electronic number tells people where they stand. It does not shorten the wait. The fix is deciding who goes where, and when to open or move a counter.

What QueuePulse does

Customers arrive at different times with different service needs, including priority cases, across three to eight counters. Open the simulator.

Predicts congestion
